NUH anaesthesia roster: quick guide

One page to keep beside the OT computer. The app: nuhroster.anawj.com · Practice (made-up staff, nothing real touched): nuhroster.anawj.com/?demo

Back to the roster

Each morning, for tomorrow

  1. Monthly rosters: check they're loaded for this month and next (✓ chips). Leave, MC and post call come from them.
  2. Cases: "Do it all" with the admin team's draft. It reads the running rooms, case notes and leave, then makes the roster.
  3. Roster: fix each Problem with its button (Take off, Fill empty gaps, Swap for someone suitable). "Check" items are worth a look.
  4. Premeds: a junior who wasn't around yesterday needs premed cover.
  5. Send it out: Download (Excel, PDF, image for WhatsApp, or the image in 2 parts) or Save for the team. After a late change, "Send image with the changes".

Changing things

  1. Tap a name for its box: leaving time, (L), comment, Move or swap.
  2. + adds someone (best fits first); × takes them off.
  3. Several days of leave or admin: Monthly › Today, tap them, "Same until".
  1. Undo (Ctrl+Z) takes back anything until you change the date.
  2. Names in red aren't on the staff list: "Fix names" remembers the spelling.
  3. Staff check their own day on My day, and can add it to their calendar.
